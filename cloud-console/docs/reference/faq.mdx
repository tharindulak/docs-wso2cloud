## Accounts and organizations

### What is the difference between my account and my organization?

Your account is you, the person who signs in. Your organization is the tenant that holds users, service contacts, and resources. See [Key concepts](../get-started/key-concepts.mdx).

### Can I belong to more than one organization?

You can create one organization for now. You can join other organizations when their administrators invite you. See [Organizations](../guides/organizations.mdx#one-organization-per-user).

### How do I sign in?

With your Google or GitHub account, or with your organization's identity provider if an admin has set up enterprise sign-in. See [Sign up and create your organization](../get-started/sign-up.mdx) and [Set up enterprise sign-in](../guides/enterprise-sign-in.mdx).

### Where are my projects, APIs, and agents?

Projects are shared across services, so you see the same projects in every service except Platform Engineering, which keeps its own. Components, such as agents, integrations, and APIs, are managed in the service you built them with. See [Key concepts](../get-started/key-concepts.mdx#projects) and [Services](../index.mdx#services).

## Users and access

### How do I add people to my organization?

Invite them by email and choose the group they join. See [Invite users](../guides/invite-users.mdx).

### Who does WSO2 notify about maintenance, security, and billing?

Your organization's service contacts. See [Manage service contacts](../guides/manage-service-contacts.mdx).

## Billing

### Is there a free plan?

Every service has a **Free** tier, a 30-day free trial. After the trial, subscribe to the **Enterprise** tier through AWS Marketplace. See [Subscriptions and billing](../guides/subscriptions-and-billing.mdx).

### Who do I contact about my bill?

Contact [WSO2 support](./support.mdx). To choose who receives invoices, see [Manage your subscription](../guides/manage-your-subscription.mdx).

## Regions and data

### Which regions are available?

See [Data residency and regions](../concepts/security/data-residency.mdx).

### Can I run workloads in my own cloud account?

Yes, with a private data plane. See [Data planes](../concepts/architecture/data-planes.mdx#private-data-plane-pdp).

### Can my workloads reach a database in my own network?

Yes. Connect an environment to your Tailscale network, add the database as an endpoint, and choose which projects can use it. Connections are outbound and TCP only. See [Set up private network access](../guides/private-network-access.mdx).

## Observability

### Can WSO2 Cloud tell me when a component has a problem?

Yes. Create an alert rule on the component, for example for high CPU usage or error logs. When the rule fires, WSO2 Cloud sends an alert to a webhook or to email addresses. See [Alerts](../guides/observability/alerts.mdx).

### Who can set up alerts?

Admins, Platform Engineers, and Developers can create alert rules. Only Admins and Platform Engineers can set up the notification channels that receive alerts. See [Alerts](../guides/observability/alerts.mdx#who-can-use-alerts).
